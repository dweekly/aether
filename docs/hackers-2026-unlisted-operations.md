# Hackers 2026: the 372 training operations missing from our school list

Generated by `research/FlightSchoolMapping/scripts/55_unlisted_schools.py --doc` from `data/research-2026-11/unlisted/` (workstream 4 of [the research plan](hackers-2026-research-plan.md)). Regenerate rather than edit.

## What was done

- The list: 372 operations (1,931 aircraft): non-individual FAA registrants with at least three aircraft that fly like trainers (training score >= 0.7) and no tail on any school fleet we know (`40_training_detect.py`, release v0.3-20261023). Rebuilt from `training_scores.parquet`, `aircraft.parquet` and `schools_full.json`; identical, tail for tail, to `training.json`.
- Discovery: Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the server-side web search tool, given the registrant's name, type and address, its home airports, main models and up to twelve tails, returned a website (the registrant's own, or the school or club that flies a leasing entity's aircraft), its evidence and an operation type (`55a_unlisted_discover.py`).
- Crawl: every website found, with the March HTTP crawler (`09a_fleet_crawl.py --input --output-dir`), and the March Playwright crawler (`10_fleet_browser_crawl.py --crawl-only --output-dir`) for every site the HTTP crawl flagged or could not fetch (`55b_unlisted_crawl.py`).
- Extraction: `extract_v2.py` (the March prompt, unchanged) with Claude Haiku 5.5 at effort medium on the Message Batches API.
- Scoring: for each site, how many of the registrant's tails (every aircraft the registry lists for it) appear on the crawled pages as written, or among the aircraft the extraction read off them. At least one is **confirmed**. A site with page text and none is **unconfirmed**, not a match; a site that returned no page text is **unreachable**.

## Headline

- 255 of 372 operations (69%) have a website the search could tie to them (245 distinct sites).
- 62 are confirmed (24% of those with a website, 17% of all 372): at least one of the registrant's own tails is on the site. For 165 the site lists none of them; for 28 the site could not be read.
- Confirmed schools and clubs: 53 operations on 51 sites. **13 of them (13 sites) were missed entirely by the March web search**: their domain is in none of the March directory, AOPA, Cirrus, expansion or crawl lists. The other 40 were on a site we already knew, whose March fleet extraction did not carry these tails: for 38 of them March read no tails off the site at all.
- Counting unconfirmed ones too, 42 operations the search called a school or club have a site that was on none of the March lists.
- Negative control: each site searched for a different operation's tails confirms 0 of 255 (real pairing: 62).

## How the confirmations split

- Tails found by text match only: 30; by extraction only: 0; the rest by both.
- Aircraft in confirmed operations: 337.
- Hand look at the 30 text-only confirmations (first matching tail on each site, 2026-11-03): in 21 the tail is only in an image file name, alt text, a POH or checklist link or a page URL; in 9 it is in visible text the extraction did not return as a fleet entry (headings such as "Piper Warrior N43337", a club's insurance note, a blog post, a for-sale listing).
- A confirmation ties the registrant to the site; it does not by itself make the operation a school. Two confirmed sites list the tail as an aircraft for sale (an avionics shop and a dealer's inventory page).

## Cost

- discovery: $13.50, extract_http: $1.66, extract_http_cancelled_low: $1.10, extract_browser: $0.14, extract_browser_cancelled_low: $0.10; total $16.49. Discovery is recorded usage at list prices plus $10 per 1,000 searches; extraction is recorded usage at batch prices. The `_cancelled_low` lines are a first submission at effort low, cancelled after the workstream 1 re-run found that Haiku 5.5 at low lists one entry per aircraft type on big fleet tables; most of it had already run.
- Discovery settings: the first 20 operations (the largest) ran at effort medium with up to 4 or 5 searches and cost $1.37; the other 352 ran at effort low with up to 3 searches, to fit the $15 budget.

## Caveats

- Unconfirmed is not wrong: many school sites list makes and models without tails. It means this check could not tie the site to the registrant's aircraft.
- A confirmed site can be the operating school for a leasing LLC (`website_relation` in `scores.json`); the operation is then real training, but the registrant is not itself a school.
- Operation types and websites come from one model pass over web search results; the evidence text and the URLs the search returned are kept per operation in `discovery.jsonl`.
- "Missed by the March search" compares registrable domains (the last two labels) against every website the March directory, AOPA, Cirrus, expansion and crawl lists held.
