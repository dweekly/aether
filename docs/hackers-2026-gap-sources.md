# Three more public sources vs our flight-school list

Generated by `research/FlightSchoolMapping/scripts/65_gap_sources.py`; do not edit by hand.

> **Headline.** Together the three point to about 167 training operations missing from our 5,458 on strict evidence (about 269 counting weak evidence), most of them from Tach & Go. Tach & Go: 1,653 of its 2,086 training-relevant listings are already in our list; of the 433 that are not, a hand check finds 10 of 30 to be real training operations we lack on their own evidence (another 7 only by an AirNav checkbox or bare listing), so about 144 (83–222). PPP NAICS 611512: 308 unmatched organizations, 2 of 30 new and operating, so about 21. NASR: 792 airports flag instruction or rental with no school of ours, but at 18 of the 23 with the strongest ADS-B training signal the school *is* in our list, filed under the wrong airport or none; 2 were new (1 more on weak evidence).

> **Correction to a working assumption.** The NASR gap measures our airport assignments as much as our coverage: wrong or missing `locations` (KPWT for Weatherford TX, KAVL for Greenville NC, Tucson only for Epic Flight Academy's New Smyrna Beach main base) hide schools we already have.

## Sources and method

- **School list**: `output/flight_line.json`, 5,458 schools. A school's airports are its listed `locations` plus `inferred_locations` (airports home to its fleet's tails, `scripts/infer_airports.py`). Identifiers are normalized through `scripts/airport_index.py canonical_ids`, so KSQL and SQL are the same airport.
- **ADS-B**: release `v0.4-20261105-rc4` (`aircraft.parquet` home airports, `training_scores.parquet` from `40_training_detect.py`). A score of 0.5 or more is that model's training flag; its precision against website fleets is at least 24% (a lower bound, because unlabeled aircraft include schools we never found). 'New' flagged aircraft are not on any school's fleet list.
- **FAA NASR**: 28-day subscription APT_CSV, effective 2026/10/29 (`nfdc.faa.gov/webContent/28DaySub/extra/29_Oct_2026_APT_CSV.zip`; nfdc.faa.gov's robots.txt allows `/webContent/`). `OTHER_SERVICES` is self-reported by the airport to the FAA; `LAST_INFO_RESPONSE` is when it last answered.
- **Tach & Go** (SyncEtc LLC): `150thaero.org/directory`, one static page per state, filter pages (`?type=school`, `rental`, `club`, `?tag=soaring`) and a page per listing for its website. robots.txt disallows only `/feeds/`; the Customer and User Terms (updated 2026-10-14) say listings show name, kind, airport, website and offerings and contain no rule against reading the public directory. Fetched one request every 4 s with an identified user agent (`aether-flight-school-research/1.0 (david@weekly.org)`).
- **SBA PPP**: the FOIA loan files on data.sba.gov (`public_150k_plus_240930.csv` and twelve `public_up_to_150k_*_240930.csv`), filtered to `NAICSCode` 611512 (Flight Training). Borrowers are de-duplicated by normalized name and ZIP. Sole proprietors, independent contractors, self-employed people and any name without a business word are *individuals*: counted, never named here.
- **Matching**: `probes/match_external_school_lists.py` (domain, exact name, street address, fuzzy name within a state), then the stricter `confirmed_match` from `59_google_places.py` (websites agree, or the names share every distinctive word and are nearly the same). A loose match that fails the strict rule is *unconfirmed* and is treated as possibly new.
- **Homepages**: unmatched entries with a website get one fetch of the homepage: live, blocked (401/403/429/503, nothing tries to get past them) or down; training words on the page; parked-domain or 'we have closed' text.
- **Hand checks**: a seeded random sample (`random.Random(65)`) of 30 unmatched entries per source, each looked up on its own website or by web search, never AOPA. Labels and evidence URLs are in `docs/hackers-2026-gap-labels.csv`.

**How complete the flag is.** Of the 1,685 US NASR airports where our list *lists* a school, 1,329 (79%) carry INSTR or RNTL. ### The gap

- 1,351 of the 2,143 flagged airports have a school in our list (1,329 by listed airport, 22 more only by inferred airport).
- **792 flagged airports have no school in our list** (772 list INSTR, 381 RNTL; 60 are private-use).
- 13 gap airports are home to at least one tail on some school's fleet (15 tails), which usually means a school at a neighboring field keeps aircraft there.
- 133 are home to at least one training-flagged aircraft that is on no school's fleet; 23 to three or more scoring 0.7 or higher.
- 465 are home to no aircraft at all in the release (it assigns a home airport to 93,311 aircraft at 4,426 airports, so small fields often have none).
- 139 have a Tach & Go school, club or rental listing at the same airport; at **56** that listing strictly matches a school *in our list*, which our list files under a different airport (or none).
- 168 are in a city where a school in our list has its contact address.
- **Unexplained gap**: 590 airports have none of those three explanations (a matched Tach & Go listing, a school address in the city, a school tail based there); 66 of them have a training-flagged aircraft on no fleet.
- Median distance to the nearest airport with a school in our list: 19.6 nm; 138 are within 10 nm.
- When the FAA last heard from these airports: last data response 2024-2026 22, 2020-2023 25, 2000-2019 22, before 2000 94, none on file 629; last inspection 2024-2026 658, 2020-2023 75, 2000-2019 18, before 2000 23, none on file 18.
