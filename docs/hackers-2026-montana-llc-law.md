# Montana-LLC aircraft: the law, and who enforces it

Research for the talk's Montana beat (2026-11-06). Gemini (gemini-4-argon-high, via Antigravity) did a broad primary-source search. Its full notes, with every citation it found, are kept outside the repo in `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/30e7de17-ac0d-46d3-a67f-dd9d8030c9cc/implementation_plan.md`. Only the items below were checked by us. Anything else from that file is unverified.

> **Headline.** Registering a plane to a Montana LLC is legal. Use tax is owed where the plane is actually kept. Idaho has twice upheld use tax on Montana-LLC aircraft. In 2026 California both charged people criminally over Montana-LLC cars and passed a law that taxes a shell LLC's in-state members. States enforce this with exactly the joins we used: the FAA registry, flight tracking and insurance data.

## What it means for the talk

- Safe to say: it's legal to register there; the tax is owed where the plane lives; states already catch this with the same public joins (Idaho with FlightAware days, Utah with insurance data); and California made the LLC's members liable this year.
- Our numbers are a screen, not evidence about anyone. We name no aircraft or owners.
