# Google Places vs our flight-school list

Generated by `research/FlightSchoolMapping/scripts/59_google_places.py`; do not edit by hand.

> **Headline.** Text searches tiling the US found 11,420 places; 4,527 are operating US places Google labels as flight training. 1,993 match a school in our list (website, or a near-identical name: 212 looser name or address matches were set aside as unmatched); of the 2,534 that don't, 900 have a live website that talks about flight training; after dropping 76 model-aircraft, paragliding, simulator, high-school and social-page entries and collapsing repeated websites, 771 remain.

## Method

- Queries: flight school, flight training, flying club, flight instruction, aviation academy, flight lessons, helicopter flight training, glider rides and instruction. Each runs over rectangles tiling the US, split in four whenever a rectangle returns Google's 60-result maximum. IDs-only text search is free.
- Place Details Pro for every place found (name, Maps category label, address, status); a place counts as flight training when its label matches `flight school|flying club|aero ?club|flight instructor|aviation training|pilot school|aircraft rental|\bglider|(?<!hang )(?<!para)gliding|soaring`.
- Matched against `output/flight_line.json` with `probes/match_external_school_lists.py`; websites (Place Details Enterprise) fetched only for unmatched places, then matched again by domain.
- Each unmatched place's website checked with `56_fsai_directory.check_site` (live, mentions flight training). Google content other than place IDs stays out of the repo.

## What skipping single-query places misses

Details were fetched for the 6,977 places found by at least 2 queries and a random 300 of the other 4,443 (seed 20261104). 30 of those 300 (10.0%) are labelled as flight training, so about 414 more such places sit among the 4,143 not fetched.

Those 4,143 were then fetched too: 388 are labelled as flight training, so the sample's estimate held.

## Hand check

A random 30 of the 771 (Python random.seed(2026) over the list below) were read by hand (`docs/hackers-2026-google-sample.csv`): 16 are flight training we did not have, 7 are not flight training (charter, maintenance, software, directories, paramotor, simulator), 1 already in our list, 6 unclear. Precision is 53% to 73%, so somewhere around 411 to 565 of these are new. Keyword checks on a homepage cannot separate an FBO or charter operator that mentions training from a school, so the added-schools step classifies each site with a model and is hand-checked again.
